# Features

## Core Administration
- Conscript registry (create/edit/delete/restore)
- Officer and department management
- Work assignment management

## Attendance & Daily Completion
- Attendance calendar
- Leave tracking and alerts
- Daily completion reports with Excel export

## Soldier Notes
- Notebook entries with categories
- Attachments, preview, download
- History export

## Alerts & Notifications
- Alarms with recurrence and history
- Notifications module
- Real-time updates via SignalR

## Analytics
- KPIs by department/officer
- State, qualification, governorate, religion breakdowns
- Radif date analytics with exclusion filters

## Backup & Recovery
- Manual and scheduled backups
- Data snapshot exports
